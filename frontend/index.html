Corporate Profile

Architecting Strategic Platforms for B2B Growth & Industry Collaboration

Saina Exhibitions Pvt. Ltd. (SEPL) is a leading professional organization specializing in organizing large-scale B2B exhibitions, trade shows, business conferences, executive CXO summits, and industry awards.

[Official Company Description: Saina Exhibitions Pvt. Ltd. is committed to fostering robust trade relationships, facilitating direct sourcing networks, and providing platforms where manufacturers, wholesalers, exporters, and retail giants convene to unlock sustainable business opportunities across multiple commercial sectors.]

With extensive domain expertise in the apparel, garment, fashion, textile, and manufacturing ecosystems, SEPL drives innovation and business growth through its flagship exhibitions and digital media publications.

Industry Focus: Apparel, Garments, Textiles, Sourcing, and B2B Manufacturing.
Professional Positioning: Seamless end-to-end event execution with nationwide buyer and exhibitor outreach.
Integrated Ecosystem: Seamless blend of on-ground expos, executive roundtables, and ongoing digital media channels.
OUR PROGRESSION

Our Journey

A strategic timeline of sustainable growth, industry trust, and platform expansion.

Stage 01

Company Foundation

Establishment of Saina Exhibitions Pvt. Ltd. with a core mission to bridge industry gaps and create dependable B2B commercial platforms.

Stage 02

Industry Development

In-depth research, association partnerships, and cultivating deep relationships with manufacturers, regional trade bodies, and institutional buyers.

Stage 03

Major Exhibitions

Launch and successful staging of landmark exhibitions including the Garment Show of India (GSI), welcoming thousands of industry participants.

Stage 04

Platform Expansion

Diversifying event formats to encompass specialized sourcing expos like TAFS Show, high-profile CXO meets, and industry excellence awards.

Stage 05

Digital Media & Content

Establishing continuous 365-day value through Perfect Sourcing publications, digital content channels, video features, and real-time market insights.
